# Answers to each section of the Round 2 brief

Prototype figures are synthetic; market figures come from Round 1 research.

## 1. Manufacturer segmentation

11,645 manufacturers can carry a real price gap.

| | A · Offline, B2B only | B · Online elsewhere | C · Churned from Meesho |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share | 72% (≈8,400) | 19% (≈2,200) | 9% (≈1,050) |
| Who | Made to order for distributors, no returns, 45–60 day terms | On Amazon, Flipkart or own D2C; GST and catalogue done | Listed, quit within two quarters |
| Barrier | No unit-level operations; stocking ahead is a balance-sheet change | ₹800+ catalogue loses money at a ₹265 AOV | Weak first 30 orders, then a returns or RTO shock |
| Motivation | Idle capacity; US tariff shock (50%, Aug–Dec 2025) | 264 mn buyers and Tier-2+ reach | Catalogue and KYC already done |
| Lever | Factory Node + pre-order batches | Price check + separate value-tier range, not discounts | Returns firewall + health-score re-entry |
| Reach | Cluster associations, 40–60 factories a drive | Price-check link via seller agencies | Meesho's dormant-seller list, one WhatsApp re-invite |

Start with Cohort A in Tiruppur and Panipat: four of six barriers are severe and all four are fixable with infrastructure.

Supporting evidence (ICRIER 2025, 2,365 manufacturing MSMEs): about one-fifth of non-integrated firms had tried e-commerce and quit; top reasons were lack of knowledge (43%), product returns (43%), high platform charges (42%). Full list in `10_facts_and_sources.md`.

## 2. Market sizing and opportunity

**Funnel:** 4.72 cr Udyam MSMEs → 5.28 lakh above micro → 1.85 lakh manufacturers → 55,451 in Meesho's categories → 19,408 own ≥ 3 of 4 cost levers → **11,645 in 12 clusters** → **1,400 active by Year 4** (12%).

| | Pilot (D90) | Scale (Q2+) | Year 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sellers | 60 | 450 | 1,400 |
| NMV / year | ₹86 cr | ₹641 cr | ₹1,995 cr |
| Share of FY26 NMV | 0.2% | 1.5% | 4.8% |
| Buyer savings / year | ₹7–12 cr | ₹56–87 cr | ₹173–272 cr |
| Contribution at 4.0% | ₹3.4 cr | ₹26 cr | ₹80 cr |

Buyer savings = NMV × g ÷ (1 − g), g = 8–12%. NMV assumes the Round 1 yield of ₹1.43 cr per seller. Why now: rivals' fees are converging to zero (Flipkart 0% on all fashion since 8 Jul 2026), so cost structure is the only price edge a rate card can't copy.

## 3. Onboarding model

Six steps: price check → Demand Brief → batch window → bulk handover at the Factory Node (30% prepaid) → node runs unit operations → settlement 7 days after delivery.

**Risk-sharing:**

| Risk | B2B today | With C2M |
|---|---|---|
| Unsold stock | None | Only the 15% buffer |
| Returns | None | Node handles them; firewall pool caps losses |
| Waiting for cash | 45–60 days | 30% upfront, rest ~7 days after delivery |
| Demand | Distributor orders | Pre-orders + district data |

**Assisted operations:** the Factory Node; bulk freight and shorter returns also lower cost (13.3% vs 10.8% below market for the example seller).

## 4. Sustainable scale-up

- **Early conviction in visibility, not money:** starter impressions in brief districts; +25,000 impressions for 7 days if orders < 60% of cohort median at D7 or D14 (max 2); C2M capped at 20% of category impressions.
- **Retention by rules:** daily Health Score drives automatic fixes; a person only after a fix fails for 7 days.
- **Graduation:** score ≥ 80 for 30 days → prepaid batches; long term, Meesho Mall brands.
- **Why it scales:** 90 automatic vs 3 human actions in the 60-seller pilot ≈ 910 sellers per category manager; ~2 reviewers for 1,400 sellers vs ~70 account managers.
- **Rejected:** order subsidies, account manager per seller, onboarding by size, weakening the quality gate.

## 5. Metrics and KPIs

**North star:** NMV from C2M sellers whose catalogue still clears the ≥ 8% gate.

| Onboarding | Activation | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified → listed ≥ 50% in 30 days (weekly · Category) | Orders at D7/D14 ≥ 60% of cohort median (daily · Data science) | Active at D90 ≥ 75% (monthly · Category) |
| First listing ≤ 7 days (weekly · Supplier product) | First-30-day orders ≥ cohort p50 for 70% (weekly · Growth) | Gate retention D14/D30 ≥ 70% (weekly · Pricing) |
| First batch ≤ 14 days (weekly · Category) | 10th order ≤ 10 days (weekly · Growth) | Graduated by D60 ≥ 40% (monthly · Finance) |

Health Score weights: price gap 30%, order pace 25%, quality returns 20%, on-time dispatch 15%, in-stock 10%. Guardrails: category NMV ≥ pre-period; badged ≤ 20% of impressions; quality returns ≤ 1.5× norm; escalations ≤ 5 per 100 sellers a month. Review cadence: daily rules, weekly KPI review, monthly recalibration.

## 6. Roadmap (30-60-90)

| When | What | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| D0–30 (₹0 capex) | Price test with holdout; Health Score live | Lift ≥ 12% (interval > 0) and ≥ 70% still clear at D14 |
| D31–60 | Tiruppur and Panipat associations; 60 factories; briefs in 5 categories | 2 associations, 60 factories |
| D61–90 | Batch window + prepayment; first partner-warehouse node | 3 of 4: batches in SLA, returns end at node, retention ≥ 70%, category NMV steady |
| Q2+ | Wave 2: 5 clusters, 6 nodes, 450 sellers; Wave 3 (Y2–4): 12 clusters, 1,400 | Each wave opens only if the previous wave's Day-90 numbers hold |

Money: ₹0 to D30, ₹12 cr by D90 only if gates pass, ₹22 cr Year 1; NPV +₹156 cr, breakeven Year 3 (Round 1). Power: 62 sellers detect a ~4.5% lift at 80% power, well under the 12% bar.

## 7. Risks and guardrails

| Risk | Guardrail |
|---|---|
| Price gap was a promotion | Day-30 stop rule; badge off at < 4% gap |
| Loss-leader SKUs game the badge | ≥ 60% of live SKUs must clear |
| Quality falls with price | Suspend + QC at 1.5× norm returns |
| Re-rank starves resellers | Badged ≤ 20% of category impressions |
| Capex before demand | No node before the D30 and D60 gates; partner warehouses first |
| Export recovery pulls factories back | Batches fill idle capacity around export runs |
| Rate-card war | Compete on cost structure, not fees |
| Shein-style squeeze (336 signed, 1 left) | Never ask for a price cut; reward only an existing gap |
| Benchmark moves as C2M lowers prices | Benchmark against resellers only once C2M share is large (**not yet in the deck**) |

Compliance by design: seller keeps title at the node (Press Note 2); badge declared as a ranking parameter and grants never sold (E-Commerce Amendment Rules, in force 1 Jan 2027); pre-order discount shown up front (dark-pattern guidelines); district-level data only (DPDP Act); node registered as the seller's additional place of business (GST); prepayment via an NBFC partner.

Still to verify before capex: return fee (₹90–150), node handling cost, pre-order uptake, order lift from grants.
