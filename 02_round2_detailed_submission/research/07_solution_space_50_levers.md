# The full solution space: 50 levers

Every realistic lever for the C2M problem, grouped by the part of the brief it solves. **Ours** = in our plan · **Add** = worth adding · **Maybe** = works with a catch · **No** = rejected (reason given).

## 1. Finding the right manufacturers (big ≠ cheap)

| # | Lever | How it works | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price Truth Index | Badge sellers whose products really sell cheaper than market | Ours |
| 2 | Cost-ownership screen | Score on raw material, production, scale, automation | Ours (category and cluster level) |
| 3 | Cluster scoring (AHP) | Rank clusters on cost, demand, returns, node feasibility, associations | Ours |
| 4 | Reverse sourcing | Find products sold by many resellers (same photos, same item), trace back to the factory, sign it directly | Add |
| 5 | GST invoice signals | Makers buy raw material and sell in bulk; traders buy finished goods; visible in GST filings with consent | Maybe: needs consent and data access |
| 6 | Udyam / industry codes | Separate makers from traders by registration codes | Ours (sizing) |
| 7 | Onboard by turnover | Pick the biggest sellers | No: the brief's own warning; 7 of 10 largest fail our screen |

## Top additions and the research each needs

| Add | Why | Research |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse sourcing (#4) | Targets factories behind best-selling reseller products | Check 20 top hosiery and home-textile listings for duplicates; trace a few to their factory |
| Group buying into batches (#31) | Makes pre-orders something buyers want | Survey 30–50 buyers: wait 7 days for ₹20–40 off with friends? |
| Reseller push (#32) | Free demand from Meesho's network | Talk to 5–10 active resellers about sharing C2M products |
| Middleman as partner (#12) | Removes Cohort A's distributor fear | Ask the interviewed factories |
| Working-capital loans (#26) | Retention without subsidy | Review existing platform-based seller lending in India |
| Resellers-only benchmark (#46) | Answers "what happens when everyone's cheaper?" | Design fix; no field work |
