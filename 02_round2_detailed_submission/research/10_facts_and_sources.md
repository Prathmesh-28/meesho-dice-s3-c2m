## Regulation

| Rule | What it requires | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DPIIT Press Note 2 (2018), effective 1 Feb 2019 | FDI marketplaces may not own or control seller inventory (a vendor is deemed controlled if > 25% of its purchases come from the marketplace or its group); fair, non-discriminatory services; no exclusivity | [Khaitan & Co](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/FDI-in-E-commerce-activities-press-note-no-2), [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=20ee8558-3d00-42f2-b1bf-9bf26b898728) |

| E-Commerce (Amendment) Rules 2026: notified 9 Sep 2026 (G.S.R. 789(E)), in force 1 Jan 2027 | Ranking parameters in descending order of importance; sponsored listings clearly labelled; dark-pattern compliance with a yearly self-audit and certificate; seller details shown; seller name on invoice in the same font size as the platform's | [LexOrbis](https://www.lexorbis.com/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-key-changes-for-online-platforms-and-sellers/) |
