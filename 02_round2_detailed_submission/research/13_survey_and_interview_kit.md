# Primary research kit: buyer survey, manufacturer pulse, interview notes

For the Factory Direct deck (page 2 "Research" and the model's adoption and price-response inputs). Built to the csuite-case-deck research protocol: behaviour in a recall window, no leading or conditional questions, a rupee amount on every incentive question, one idea per question, base n kept for every question.

**Deadline:** responses and notes by **Sat 3 Oct, 8 PM IST**, so the model and page 2 can be rebuilt and audited before the 4 Oct, 11:59 PM submission.

---

## A. Buyer survey (Google Form, ~4 minutes, English + Hindi labels)

**Who:** people who bought on Meesho in the last 3 months. **Target:** n ≥ 150 overall, ≥ 30 in each city-tier group you report (metro, tier-2, tier-3 or smaller). Share via family, hostel staff, home-town and community WhatsApp groups, not only classmates. **Pilot** with 5 people before sharing.

| # | Question | Options | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where do you live? | Metro (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) · Other city · Small town · Village | Quota, segment |
| 2 | How many orders did you place on Meesho in the last 3 months? | 0 (end survey) · 1–2 · 3–5 · 6 or more | Screener |
| 3 | What did you buy? (tick all) | Clothes · Innerwear / socks · Bedsheets / towels · Kitchen / home items · Footwear · Jewellery · Bags · Other | Category demand |
| 4 | What was the price of your last Meesho order? | Under ₹200 · ₹200–399 · ₹400–699 · ₹700 or more | Order value check vs ₹265 AOV |
| 5 | How did you pay for your last Meesho order? | Cash on delivery · UPI / card / wallet in advance | Prepaid share (RTO lever) |
| 6 | Your next order is a ₹250 item. Which delivery would you choose? | Delivered in 3–4 days, ₹250 · Delivered in 7–8 days, ₹240 | Price response at ₹10 |
| 7 | Same item. Which would you choose? | 3–4 days, ₹250 · 7–8 days, ₹230 | Price response at ₹20 |
| 8 | Same item. Which would you choose? | 3–4 days, ₹250 · 7–8 days, ₹210 | Price response at ₹40 |
| 9 | If you chose 7–8 days, how would you pay? | In advance (UPI/card) · Cash on delivery · I would not choose 7–8 days | Prepaid pre-orders |
| 10 | A listing says "Sold directly by the manufacturer". Compared with a normal listing, how much do you trust its quality? | More · Same · Less | Badge value |
| 11 | What would stop you choosing a 7–8 day option? (pick up to 2) | Need it sooner · Worry it won't arrive · Can't cancel · Want reviews first · Price difference too small · Nothing would stop me · Other | Barriers |
| 12 | Your age and gender (optional) | 18–24 · 25–34 · 35–44 · 45+ ; Woman · Man · Prefer not to say | Quotas |

**Do not change:** the rupee amounts (they build the price-response curve), or the neutral wording (no "fast and convenient").

**Export:** raw counts per option and the base n for each question (Responses → Download CSV).

## B. Manufacturer pulse (5 minutes, for factory owners you can reach: Round 1 interviewees, their peers, cluster associations)

| # | Question | Options |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cluster and product | Free text |
| 2 | Annual turnover | Under ₹10 cr · ₹10–50 cr · ₹50–100 cr · Over ₹100 cr |
| 3 | Spare capacity in a normal month | Under 10% · 10–20% · 20–30% · Over 30% |
| 4 | Have you sold online directly before? | Never · Yes, still do · Yes, but stopped |
| 5 | If stopped or never: top 2 reasons (mirrors the ICRIER list) | Don't know how · Returns · Platform charges · Competition · No tech/inventory skills · Not profitable · No staff · Not enough stock |
| 6 | Smallest batch you would make for a confirmed order | Under 200 · 200–500 · 500–1,000 · Over 1,000 units |
| 7 | Which ONE would most change your answer about selling on Meesho? | Someone else packs, ships and handles returns · Orders confirmed before I produce · 30% paid at handover · Weekly demand data by district · None |
| 8 | Payment terms you get from distributors today | Under 30 days · 30–45 · 45–60 · Over 60 days |

Even 10–15 responses are useful: report as "x of y owners", never as a percentage of all manufacturers.
