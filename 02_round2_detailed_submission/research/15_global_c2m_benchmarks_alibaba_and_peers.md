# Factory-direct (C2M) programmes: Alibaba and the other players

Deep research for the Factory Direct deck, 3 Oct 2026. Covers what Alibaba, Pinduoduo/Temu, JD, Shein, Douyin, Amazon, Walmart/Flipkart, ONDC, Udaan and IndiaMART have done to sell factory goods direct to consumers, what worked, what failed, and what Meesho should copy.

**How to read the numbers**
- Every figure carries a date and a source (listed at the end).
- Figures from a company's own filing or press release are marked **(company)**. Figures from press or analysts are marked **(press)**.
- Where a target has no published result, the text says so.

---

## 1. The short answer

1. **Alibaba ran the most complete factory-direct experiment.** It built a factory app (Taobao Deals, 2020), a wholesale base of 1 million factories (1688), a 100-piece smart factory (Xunxi) and a product-design lab (TMIC).
   - The part that lasted is the **plumbing**: demand data to factories, small-batch production, and cluster programmes.
   - The part that did **not** last is **paying for shoppers**: Taobao Deals spent heavily on user acquisition, hit about 300 mn buyers, then cut acquisition spend in 2022 and later folded into Taobao.
2. **Every winner gave factories something before asking for anything:** demand data (Pinduoduo, Alibaba, JD), small-batch production (Shein, Xunxi), or finance (Alibaba). **The one clear failure asked for price cuts:** Shein in Brazil kept 1 of 336 factories.
3. **Seller-owned stock beats platform-owned stock on profit.** In the US, Temu's semi-managed model (seller holds stock) earns about **+4%**; its fully managed model (Temu holds stock) runs at about **−5%**. Semi-managed is now about 70% of Temu's US business. This supports Factory Direct's rule that the factory keeps title at the node.
4. **Clusters are the right unit.**
   - China counted **7,780 "Taobao villages"** in 2022.
   - **1688 is present in 174 of China's 192 top industrial belts.**
   - Douyin reports **830 industrial belts** each selling over ¥100 mn.
   - Recruiting through Tiruppur and Panipat matches how China scaled.
5. **A tariff shock is the moment to move.** In April–May 2025, China's platforms rushed to help exporters hit by US tariffs sell at home:
   - JD committed ¥200 bn to buy their goods.
   - Taobao/Tmall pledged help to 10,000 exporters.
   - 20,000+ exporters were listed within weeks.

   This is the same window Tiruppur and Panipat are in now.
6. **The competition for India's factories has started.**
   - Shein and Reliance are growing Shein India's supplier base from **150 to 1,000** Indian manufacturers, on **100-unit first batches**.
   - Amazon's Bazaar charges **zero referral fees** on items under ₹600.
   - Flipkart charges 0% on all fashion.
   - If Meesho does not lock in cost-advantaged factories, someone else will.

## 2. Alibaba: six programmes

| Programme | What it does | Key numbers (date) | What happened | Lesson for Meesho |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Taobao C2M** (Mar 2020) | Gives factories consumer insight, product design help and finance, so they make what buyers want | Targets: **10 bn new orders** to factories in 3 years; **1,000 "super factories"** above ¥100 mn output each; **10 industrial clusters** above ¥10 bn each (Mar 2020) **(company, via Xinhua)** | No published tally of the 10 bn-order target was found | Pair demand data with finance: our **Demand Brief + NBFC advance** |
| **Taobao Deals / Taote** (Mar 2020; renamed Taote May 2021) | A separate value app selling factory-direct (M2C) goods to price-sensitive and lower-tier buyers | Annual buyers: **100 mn+** (Dec 2020) → **240 mn+** (Sep 2021) → **280 mn** (Dec 2021) → **300 mn+** (Mar 2022) **(company)**. Paid GMV of M2C products: **+40%** (Jun-22 quarter), **+35%** (Dec-22 quarter) **(company)** | By Sep 2022 Alibaba cut acquisition spend and switched to retention; analysts reported **70–80% overlap** with Pinduoduo's buyers **(press)**. Losses narrowed through 2022 **(company)**; Taote later folded into Taobao | Buying shoppers with subsidies is expensive and copyable. **Spend on factory economics (node, batches), not on buyer subsidies.** Meesho already has 274 mn buyers |
| **1688** (wholesale) | China's largest domestic wholesale marketplace; factories sell to resellers and, increasingly, through Taobao | **RMB 800 bn+** transactions in 2023; present in **174 of 192** top industrial belts (Feb 2024) **(press)**. **100 mn+** annual buyers (Feb 2025) **(press)**. Fully integrated with Taobao in 2024 **(company, 20-F)** | Now Alibaba's main supply base. In 2025 it added fee cuts, loans, subsidies and free AI tools for exporters **(press)** | Meesho's 9.6 lakh resellers are a ready demand pipe; **Kirana Club** ("Meesho for Retailers") could be Meesho's 1688 |
| **Xunxi digital factory** (Sep 2020) | Alibaba's own smart apparel factory; turns Taobao/Tmall demand data into small runs | MOQ **100 pieces**; **7 days** to produce + 3 to deliver; lead time **−75%**; inventory need **−30%**; served **~200 merchants** by Nov 2020 against a 10,000+ goal **(press)** | Became a showcase; scale stayed small | **Small batches pay off.** Our 1,000-pack batches with a 15% buffer are conservative next to a 100-piece MOQ. Don't build factories; lend the method to partners |
| **TMIC** (Tmall Innovation Center, 2017) | Shares search terms, price performance and return rates with brands to design products | **6 months** concept-to-launch vs **18–24** before; **300** incubated products; **81** brand partners (Unilever, L'Oréal, Mars …) **(press)** | Became a standard brand service | The **Demand Brief** is a TMIC for small factories: sizes, colours, price band, districts |
| **AliExpress Choice** (Mar 2023) | Cross-border; the platform handles pricing, logistics and after-sales; factories make products. A semi-managed option was added later | Over half of AliExpress orders via Choice by Jan 2024; available in **215 countries** **(company)**. Semi-managed share of orders rose from **18% to 37%** in 2025 **(press)** | Moving from fully managed to semi-managed, like Temu | Factory keeps the stock, the platform runs operations: **Factory Direct's design** |

**Two more Alibaba signals**
- **Taobao villages:** **7,780** villages with ≥ ¥10 mn a year on Taobao, plus **2,429 Taobao towns**, in 2022, together selling over **RMB 1.3 trn** (AliResearch, via academic sources). Factory e-commerce in China grew cluster by cluster, which supports our cluster AHP.
- **Export-to-domestic (Apr–May 2025):** Taobao/Tmall pledged help to **≥ 10,000 foreign-trade companies** and **100,000** export goods. Support covered:
  - simpler registration;
  - commission incentives;
  - traffic support;
  - procurement services.

  Across platforms, **20,000+ exporters** had listed by late May 2025 (China Daily). This is the closest precedent for Tiruppur and Panipat after the US tariff shock.

## 3. Pinduoduo and Temu

| Programme | Numbers (date) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **New Brand Initiative** (Dec 2018) | **1,500+** factories joined; **460 mn** cumulative orders; **2 mn+** custom items a day (Q3 2020). Target for 2021–25: 100 industrial belts, 100,000 new-brand products, ¥1 trn sales, partners from 1,000 to **5,000** (Oct 2020) **(press)**. End-2019: 900+ factories, 2,200+ custom products, 115 mn+ orders **(press, KrASIA)** | Anonymised demand data turned contract manufacturers (e.g. Sipiao tissue: 30,000+ orders in its first month) into brands. **Copy: Demand Brief** |
| **¥100 bn merchant support** (Apr 2025) | ¥100 bn over 3 years in capital, traffic and fee cuts for SMEs and industrial belts **(press)** | Funding factories directly, not buyers |
| **Xinpinmu (in-house brand)** (Mar 2026) | ¥15 bn initial injection; part of a ¥100 bn three-year plan; combines PDD and Temu supply chains; designs products with selected cluster factories **(China Daily, 26 Mar 2026)**. Reported SKU cap about **4,000** **(press)** | The next step after C2M is **factory brands**: our Year 5+ Meesho Mall path |
| **Temu: fully managed → semi-managed** | Semi-managed was **20%** of US GMV in Q3 2024; by Dec 2025 it was **~70% of US business**. Semi-managed margin about **+4%** vs fully managed about **−5%**; local-to-local still **< 5%** of GMV **(press, Tech Buzz China)** | **Seller-owned stock is the profitable model.** Platform-owned stock is barred for Meesho anyway (Press Note 2) |

## 5. Shein, including its India move with Reliance

| Item | Numbers (date) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **Test-and-reorder** | First batches of **~100–200 units**; reorders within about 5 days; **7,500** contract manufacturers, mostly SMEs; no supplier above 10% of purchases (2026 IPO filing, via Sheng Lu Fashion) | Small first batches, then scale on real sales: our **pre-order batch** |
| **Supplier investment** | **US$42 mn+** by end-2025 through its Supplier Community Empowerment Program; **US$70 mn** to 2028; **200+** supplier factories renovated, with Shein paying **40%+** of the cost **(company)** | Invest in the factory; our **node set-up support and camps** |
| **Shein Brazil** (2023–26) | ~US$150 mn pledged for 2,000 factories; **336** signed; **1** still producing by Feb 2026; factories cited ~30% price-cut demands **(Reuters)** | **Never ask a factory for a price cut** |
| **Shein India with Reliance** (Feb 2025–) | SheinIndia.in launched Feb 2025, run by Reliance. **150** Indian manufacturers, target **1,000**; first batches of **100 units**; **12,000** styles; 2.7 mn+ downloads (Jun 2025). Dresses from **₹199** **(press)** | **A direct rival for the same cost-advantaged Indian factories.** Meesho should move first in Tiruppur and Panipat |

## 6. Douyin (ByteDance)

- **Industrial-belt report, 2024:** 170 mn products from industrial-belt regions sold **15.4 bn orders** (+39%); **63%** through livestreams.
- **830 industrial belts** each sold over ¥100 mn, across 130 cities.
- **21,000** merchants each made over ¥10 mn from their own store livestreams; **1,000+** made over ¥100 mn **(company report, via press)**.
- **Lesson:** factory owners selling direct through their own content works in China. Meesho's creator and content tools could carry **factory livestreams** for C2M sellers later.

## 7. India: what rivals and the government already do

| Player | Programme | Numbers (date) | What it means for Factory Direct |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flipkart** | **Samarth** (artisans, weavers, small makers): onboarding, cataloguing, lower commission, warehousing | Supports **7 lakh+** livelihoods; sellers onboarded up 50% YoY (2024) **(press)**. 0% commission on all fashion since 8 Jul 2026 | Flipkart has the support stack for craft sellers, but **no small-batch or factory-node model** |
| **Walmart / Flipkart** | **Vriddhi** supplier development | **70,000+** MSMEs trained since 2019; **+100,000** more by 2028; graduates report ~55% YoY growth (Jun 2025) **(company)** | Training at scale exists. **Meesho's edge is demand data, batches and the node, not training** |
| **Amazon India** | Karigar, Saheli, Local Shops; **Bazaar** (value fashion) | 12 mn+ MSMEs digitised; 12 lakh+ artisans (Karigar); Bazaar: items under **₹600**, **zero referral fee** (announced Feb 2024) **(company / press)** | Amazon is moving into Meesho's price band with zero fees: fee advantages will not last, cost advantages can |
| **Shein India (Reliance)** | Small-batch sourcing from Indian factories | 150 → 1,000 manufacturers; 100-unit batches (Jun 2025) **(press)** | The closest competing model; see section 5 |
| **ONDC / MSME-TEAM** | Open network; government scheme to onboard MSMEs | 7 lakh+ sellers and service providers; ~2.06 lakh merchants with at least one transaction (Dec 2025); 1.6 cr orders in Mar 2025 **(press)**. MSME-TEAM: ₹277 cr to onboard 5 lakh MSMEs | Possible **co-funding for cluster camps**; ONDC has reach but no factory operations layer |
| **Udaan** | B2B marketplace | Revenue **₹4,561 cr** in FY25 (−20%), loss **₹1,055 cr**; moved to a regional cluster model; revenue down 54% since FY22 **(press)** | **A warning:** B2B scale without unit economics burns cash. Our gates and capped H2 budget answer this |
| **IndiaMART** | B2B lead generation | **2.2 lakh** paying suppliers; **8.7 mn** storefronts; ~₹67K a year per paying supplier (Q4 FY26) **(company)** | Manufacturers do pay for demand; a lead source for finding factories |

## 8. Pioneers and failures

| Case | What happened | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **Biyao** (China, 2014) | First pure C2M site: made-to-order goods from luxury-brand OEM factories, 2–15% take rate **(press)**. Stayed niche | Pure made-to-order with long waits suits niche buyers. **Keep the wait to 7–8 days and the discount real** |
| **NetEase Yanxuan** (2016) | ODM private label from big-brand factories; in 2018 too many SKUs led to unsold stock and quality problems **(press)** | Quality control and SKU discipline matter: our **Health Score, QC hold and catalogue gate** |
| **Taobao Deals** (2020–23) | Grew to 300 mn buyers with heavy acquisition spending, then cut spend and merged **(company / press)** | Do not subsidise buyers; Meesho already has them |
| **Shein Brazil** (2023–26) | 1 of 336 factories left after price-cut demands **(Reuters)** | Never squeeze factories |
| **Udaan** (India) | Shrank revenue to cut losses **(press)** | Gate the money; prove unit economics first |

## 9. What works, matched to Factory Direct

| What winners did | Who | Factory Direct lever | Already in the deck? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gave factories demand data | Pinduoduo, Taobao C2M, TMIC, JD | **Demand Brief** | ✓ |
| Small first batches, reorder on real sales | Shein (100–200), Xunxi (100, 7 days), Shein India (100) | **Prepaid pre-order batch** (+15% buffer) | ✓ (our batch size is larger and conservative) |
| Factory keeps stock; platform runs operations | Temu semi-managed, AliExpress Choice semi-managed | **Factory Node** (seller keeps title) | ✓ |
| Finance for factories | Taobao C2M (Alibaba's finance arms), PDD ¥100 bn | **30% NBFC advance** | ✓ |
| Recruit by cluster or belt | 1688 (174 belts), Jingxi (180+ belts), Taobao villages, Douyin (830 belts) | **Cluster camps + AHP** | ✓ |
| Rapid support when exports collapse | JD ¥200 bn; Taobao 10,000 exporters (2025) | **Pitch to export-hit Tiruppur and Panipat** | Partly: add the 2025 China precedent |
| Invest in supplier factories | Shein US$42–70 mn | **Node set-up support**; could add a factory-upgrade fund | Partly |
| Factory brands as the end state | PDD Xinpinmu, JD white-label super factories | **Year 5+ Meesho Mall path** | ✓ |
| Did **not** work: buyer subsidies | Taobao Deals | Grants are impressions, not cash | ✓ |
| Did **not** work: price squeezes | Shein Brazil | Gate measures an existing gap | ✓ |

   > "Rival locks up the factories: Shein–Reliance scaling 150 → 1,000 Indian suppliers on 100-unit batches. Guardrail: move first in Tiruppur and Panipat; association MoUs by Day 60."
4. **Why rivals cannot copy it:** add Shein India as a row (small batches ✓, value demand –, Tier-2 reach –, 0% fees ✕, node ✕, demand data to factories –).
